# Open licence questions

For AIlixir and the professorship. The matcher is a non-commercial university project for now; the [allow-list](README.md#3-indexing) is based on that. Details are on the source pages.

1. **Research use now.** May we index sources whose licence or terms allow research use (NC licences, PMC `TDM` manuscripts, arXiv's default licence) and send their text to Gemini and OpenAI for the AI summary index? Does § 60d UrhG (text and data mining for scientific research) cover all-rights-reserved works, such as the podcasts, and may we transcribe their audio for it (about $130–260 for the back catalogue with a cloud service)?
2. **What the app shows.** May the app show AI summaries of CC BY-SA, CC BY-ND, `TDM` and arXiv default-licence texts, and PubMed abstracts that are under publisher copyright?
3. **YouTube.** May Gemini summarise third-party videos from their URL? Do index rows derived from YouTube data expire after 30 days like the data itself, and does the high-risk clause (III.I.10) exclude a health app?
4. **Commercial use later.** If AIlixir uses the matcher commercially, NC sources and Semantic Scholar reference data (which includes CC BY-NC) drop out, arXiv needs its consent or its affiliate programme, and YouTube may need a quota audit. Is commercial use planned, and from when?
